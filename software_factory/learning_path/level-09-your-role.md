# Level 9: Your Role (30 min)

## Goal
Decide how (and whether) to use this on `MasjidDashboardWeb`.

## Concepts
- **Architect/manager role:** you write contracts, set boundaries, review results. Agents do the typing.
- **Seams:** places where work splits cleanly (schema, API, UI).
- **Your principles still apply:** you must understand every line. Simple first.

## Steps
1. Review your Google Doc notes from Levels 1 to 8.
2. List what worked and what felt too complex or costly.
3. Pick the smallest setup you would trust (for example: one reviewer sub agent only).
4. Write a short plan: which part of `MasjidDashboardWeb` to try it on first, and the contract for it.

## Document it
Write your decision in your Google Doc: what you will use, what you will skip, and why.

## Check yourself
- Is it fine to decide "pair programming is enough for me"? Yes. That is a valid result.
